SarkariDoc prepares the files government exam forms actually check. Photo,
signature, thumb impression, handwritten declaration, PDF. Cropped and
compressed to the exam's exact pixels and KB limit.

Every rule is cited back to the official notification, with the date I last
checked it. Free, no ads, no signup. Built for a phone.

## Why

Exam portals reject a photo that looks fine because it is 51 KB instead of 50,
or the wrong shape against a spec buried in last year's PDF. People sit in
cyber cafes guessing. I wanted one place that reads the notice and does the
resize.

## What it does

- Exam kits for SSC, UPSC, Railways, IBPS, SBI, NEET, GATE, CUET, and the
  state PSCs I have source-checked.
- Photo, signature, thumb, and handwritten declaration resizers that hit both
  pixel size and file size together.
- PDF compressors sized for the upload the form asks for.
- Calculators that follow the official rule: SSC normalisation, CGL age
  groups, caste / EWS / OBC-NCL certificate validity.
- Hindi pages where they help.

## The rule

If the notice is not linked, the page does not ship. A preset without a source
is a rumour.
